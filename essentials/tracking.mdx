Create tracking links to monitor ad and affiliate performance

From your orders page in the event dashboard, you can create tracking links that you can tie to specific ads or campaigns to track performance.

## Using Tracking Links

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a Tracking Link">
    From this page, click "Add Tracking Link" to create your first tracking link. Name the link (this is just for your reference so you know what ad/campaign it is tied to), and customize the URL (optional). Once the link is added

    <Frame>
      ![Screenshot 2026 04 10 At 11 39 44 AM](/images/Screenshot-2026-04-10-at-11.39.44-AM.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose where the link will direct">
    When you click on a link you created, you'll be prompted to select which page (tickets, tables, or event page) URL you want to copy. This allows you to use links differently based on the campaign's audience. For example, if you're running an ad for vendor tables, you can copy the tables link and paste it as the target URL for your ad

    <Frame>
      ![Screenshot 2026 04 10 At 11 55 40 AM](/images/Screenshot-2026-04-10-at-11.55.40-AM.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Plug in your URL and monitor results">
    After selecting your preferred page URL, you can paste the link directly into your campaign to start tracking activity for that link. If you're using the tracking link to review the performance of an affiliate/influencer, you can advise that person to include the unique tracking link in their bio along with a [promo code](/essentials/promo-codes) to capture the most data.
  </Step>
</Steps>
